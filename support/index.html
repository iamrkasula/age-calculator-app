Saving a birthday

On Home, set the date of birth in From and tap Calculate, then Save. Add a name, choose the event (Birthday, Anniversary or another) and a group, and tap Save. You can also open All on Home and tap Add a Date. If you don't know the year, leave it out; you can type an age instead.

Turning on reminders

In Age Calculator, open Settings → Reminders and turn on Birthday Reminders. Choose when (on the day, or 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before) and the time. Each person can use these, their own, or none. Tap Send a Test Notification to check: it arrives in 5 seconds.

Notifications don't arrive

  • Notifications are off: open the Settings app → Notifications → Age Calculator and turn on Allow Notifications. If Reminders in Age Calculator says notifications are off, tap Open iPhone Settings.
  • A Focus is on: Do Not Disturb, Sleep or another Focus can hold notifications back. Open the Settings app → Focus and allow Age Calculator, or check Notification Center later.
  • Reminders are off for a group or a person: check the group switches in Settings → Reminders, and the person's own reminder setting.
  • A new iPhone: reminders are set on each iPhone, so turn them on there too.

Adding a widget

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle. Tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Age Calculator. Pick Next Birthday, Countdown or Coming Up, then Add Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then the widget area. To choose the person on a Countdown widget, touch and hold it, then Edit Widget. These steps are also in Settings → Add a Widget.

Widgets show initials instead of names when Show Names on Lock Screen is off (Settings → Reminders).

iCloud sync between iPhones

With Settings → iCloud Sync → Sync with iCloud on, your saved dates, groups and events stay the same on every iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. A change to this switch takes effect the next time you open Age Calculator. If iCloud Sync says you're signed out, open the Settings app, tap your name and sign in to iCloud; your dates stay on the iPhone and sync again when you sign in. Reminders are set on each iPhone.

Importing from Contacts, Calendar or a file

Open Settings → Import Birthdays and choose:

  • Contacts: pick the contacts whose birthdays you want. If Age Calculator can't see your contacts, allow access in the Settings app → Age Calculator → Contacts.
  • Calendar: imports from the Birthdays calendar.
  • File: a CSV or Excel file with a name column and a date column. Dates like 2021-03-14, 03/14/2021 or March 14, 2021 work.

February 29 birthdays

In years without February 29, Age Calculator celebrates on March 1 by default. To use February 28 instead, open Settings → Feb 29 Birthdays. This setting is used for ages, countdowns, reminders and widgets.

Backup and restore

Settings → Backup & Export → Export Backup saves all your saved dates to one file; keep it in Files or send it to yourself. To bring it back, choose Restore from Backup and pick the file: it adds what's missing and never makes duplicates. Export as CSV makes a spreadsheet for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, and Add to Calendar makes yearly calendar events for every saved date.

I updated and my dates are missing

The first time it opens after the update, Age Calculator moves your saved dates over by itself, from the iPhone and from your iCloud. Your old data is kept. For 30 days after the move, Settings shows Re-run Update from 1.x: tap it to bring over anything missing. If your dates were in iCloud, make sure you're signed in to iCloud first. If that doesn't help, email us (below).
