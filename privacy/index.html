Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Age Calculator: DOB & Birthday works out exact ages and keeps a list of birthdays, anniversaries and other dates with reminders. Your saved dates never reach us. The only things that ever come to us are anonymous crash and hang reports, anonymous counts of which features are used, and emails you choose to send, all described below.

What Age Calculator does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking, across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No server of our own: your saved dates are never sent to us.

Your saved dates

On your iPhone. Names, dates, events, groups, notes, gift ideas, photos you add and reminder settings are stored on your device.

In your own iCloud. With iCloud Sync on (the default), they are also kept in your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so they stay the same on your iPhones. Apple stores this data in your iCloud account; we can't see it. You can turn sync off in Settings → iCloud Sync.

Updating from version 1. If you used an earlier version, your saved dates are moved over on your iPhone, from the old data on the device and in your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to us, and the old data is left in place.

Contacts and Calendar

Age Calculator asks for access only when you use these features, and reads them on your iPhone:

  • Import from Contacts: only the names, birthdays and photos of the contacts you pick are copied into your saved dates, which then sync like any other date.
  • Import from the Birthdays calendar: the calendar is read once, when you choose to import.
  • Message and Call: for a person linked to a contact, the phone number is read from Contacts when you tap, and is never stored.

Nothing from your contacts or calendar leaves your iPhone because of Age Calculator. You can turn access off any time in the Settings app → Age Calculator.

Reminders, widgets and Siri

Reminders are notifications scheduled and shown by your iPhone. Widgets read the app's data on your iPhone. With Show Names on Lock Screen off, widgets and reminders show initials instead of names. Siri requests such as "How old is Ava?" are answered from the data on your iPhone.

Files

Backups, CSV exports and calendar files are made on your iPhone and go only where you send them, for example Files, Mail or Messages. CSV, Excel and backup files you import are read on your iPhone.

Crash and hang reports

If the App Store version of Age Calculator crashes or stops responding, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be found and fixed. In about 10% of launches it also sends performance timings, such as how long a screen took to open.

A report contains: the device model, iOS version, app version, and the technical details of the crash or hang (the code that was running). If moving data from version 1 fails, only the type of error is sent.

A report does not contain: names, dates, notes, gift ideas, contacts, photos, screenshots or a recording of the screen. IP addresses are scrubbed and not stored.

Reports are not linked to your identity, not used for tracking or advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Anonymous usage counts

Age Calculator counts which screens and features are used, so we know what to improve. The counts go to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-first analytics service. They are always on and anonymous.

A count contains: the name of the screen or feature (for example "Date Difference opened" or "date saved: birthday, with a year"), number ranges instead of exact numbers (for example "2–5 dates imported"), and the device model, iOS version, app version and language. TelemetryDeck receives only an anonymous device ID that is hashed on the device, so the counts can't be traced back to you.

A count never contains: names, dates, ages, notes, gift ideas, contacts, photos, search text, file names or anything else you type or save.

Usage counts are not linked to your identity, not used for tracking or advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Feedback you send

Settings → Send Feedback opens the Mail app with an email addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. We use your email only to answer you and improve the app, and you can ask us to delete it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Backup & Export → Delete All Saved Dates removes your saved dates on this iPhone and, with iCloud Sync on, in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is stored on that device.

Children

Age Calculator collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
